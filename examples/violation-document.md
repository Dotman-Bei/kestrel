## The rule

A column tagged PII must not reach a BI dashboard or chart through any lineage path, unless it passes through a masking, hashing or redaction step.

```yaml
id: pii-reaches-bi
subject:   columns tagged any of PII/Sensitive/Confidential
condition: must not reach a Dashboard/Chart within 6 hops with no step matching mask|masked|hash|sha2|redact|anonymi[sz]|tokeni[sz]|pseudonym and no step tagged Masked/Anonymized
```
